Anatomy of Stored Bcrypt Hash:

Example: $2b$12$e8iVw6wG...

• $2b$: Bcrypt algorithm identifier

• $12$: Cost factor ($2^{12} = 4096$ key derivation rounds, intentionally slow)

• Next 22 chars: Cryptographically secure random salt (unique per user)

• Remaining 31 chars: Resulting hash digest
